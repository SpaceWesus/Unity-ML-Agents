# Homies: Monarch Protocol

Open `Assets/Scenes/Homies.unity` and enter Play Mode.

## Play loop

Clear escalating dungeon waves, gain Hunter levels, and convert fallen enemies into a persistent shadow squad. Every fifth wave includes a boss. Shadows fight autonomously and can be ordered to focus the target under the reticle.

## Controls

- `WASD`: move
- Mouse movement: orbit the camera and aim the center-screen reticle
- Left mouse: slash toward the reticle
- `Space` or `Left Shift`: dash
- `E`: raise the corpse nearest the reticle
- `R`: command all shadows to attack the enemy under the reticle
- `Enter`: restart after defeat

The cursor is locked and hidden while playing. Attacks include procedural sword swings, knockback, blood impacts, and sword drips. Damage to the player shakes the camera and flashes the screen red.

The feature is scene-gated, so the Turtle and racecar experiments are unchanged.
